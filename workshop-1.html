Introduction Presentation

First day of Workshop 1, gave my intro presentation which I created over the last few days. The introduction made me think about what ideas I had been listing down and thinking about over the last few months and what really interested me- tangibly in a designed form, which made it interesting, it also gave me an opportunity to show some of my photography :P

21st September 2026

Collective Research Proposal v1

v1 of the research proposal, I used numerous of ideas I had dotted down over the months to compress them into three main ideas I felt captured the same themes I felt. In v1 I created one primary element that my peers & profs said they liked: the icons. The icons came from me asking my mom and sister what those words feel like in symbols and they really worked out in a sort of system.

24th September 2026

Collective Research Proposal v2

v2 of the proposal, prof Eric said that my layouts needed more variety so I created these varying columns and messed around with my existing elements. I was told that the cover would have a half-page flap so I wrote the theme names sideways on half of the cover, and then to not repeat it on the back, I turned the three terms into a short phrase.

24th September 2026

Office Hrs with prof Christine

I had a virtual meeting with prof Christine where she gave me some insight on v2, and gave me some ideas moving forward. The meeting pushed me to gather more images from my examples, use the existing elements with more purpose, and fix some formatting errors.

28th September 2026

Collective Research Proposal v3 + Print Day

v3, the final version came together out of various ideas and variation, the cover came from an idea prof Christine gave me in office hours, I used the "timeline" lines I had with more purpose, Used my icon system with more function, and added more images in very random places. I had been told till the very end that my idea was very "all over the place" but my design style/philosophy is really organized, opposites attract :P
(also printed out pages 1 & 3 on the risograph machine, cost like 30$)
